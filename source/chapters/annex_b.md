# Annex B: Assessment thresholds

Assessment thresholds are defined in air quality legislation to determine the required type and extent of air quality assessment in each zone. They are used to determine whether fixed monitoring stations are necessary or if assessment may be based on modelling or indicative measurements.

<span id="_Toc215228808" class="anchor"></span>**Abbreviations:**

**LV**: Limit value, **TV**: Target value, **CL**: Critical level
