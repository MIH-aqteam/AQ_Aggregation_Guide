(footnote-26)=
## Footnote 26

In the specific case of CO and the assessment of a sampling point against the P1Y-daysAbove4 aggregate, values are rounded to zero decimal places. This is due to a daily average concentration of 4.1 mg/m³ is not considered to exceed the limit value of 4 mg/m³; exceedance is assumed to occur only from 4.5 mg/m³ onwards.

[↩ Return to Chapter 6 — Exceedances](06_exceedances.md)


(footnote-27)=
## Footnote 27

In the case of lead (Pb), annual mean concentrations (P1Y) are rounded to one decimal place for comparison with the limit value. A value of 0.51 µg/m³ is not considered to exceed the limit value of 0.5 µg/m³; exceedance is assumed only when the concentration reaches 0.55 µg/m³ or higher.

[↩ Return to Chapter 6 — Exceedances](06_exceedances.md)
