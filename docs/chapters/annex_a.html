Annex A: Environmental objectives and reporting metrics

Abbreviations:

LV: Limit value, TV: Target value, LTO: Long-term objective, INT: Information threshold, ALT: Alert threshold, CL: Critical level, ERT: Exposure reduction target, ECO: Exposure concentration obligation, AEI: Average exposure indicator, ERO: Exposure reduction obligations

*: At locations representative of air quality over at least 100 km² or an entire zone or agglomeration, whichever is smaller.

**: At locations representative of air quality over at least 100 km2 or an entire zone, whichever is the smaller.

*** To be attained by 1 January 2050.
